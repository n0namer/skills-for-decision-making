# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about the work, not the person.

## What is expected

- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith.
- Criticise reasoning, code and numbers as hard as you like. Not people.
- When you disagree, say what would change your mind. This repo is about decision
  quality; the same standard applies to arguments in the issue tracker.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change without it being a judgement of you.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, unwelcome attention, publishing others'
private information, or sustained disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

Report to the repository owner through a GitHub issue if it can be public, or by email if
it cannot. Reports are handled privately. Consequences range from a request to change
behaviour to a permanent ban, depending on severity and repetition.

Maintainers who do not hold themselves to this are subject to it too.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, commits, and anywhere someone is representing this
project.
